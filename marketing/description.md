# Decoherence — Drone & Soundscape Reverb

**Pluck in. Drone out.**

## Short description (store card, social posts)

Decoherence turns any sound into an endless, slowly breathing drone. Play one note and it
melts into a smooth cloud that swells, drifts and sustains for up to a minute, with no grain,
no metallic ringing and no pitch wobble you didn't ask for.

## Full description

Decoherence isn't a room reverb. It's an instrument for building drones, ambient beds and
soundscapes out of whatever you feed it. A feedback delay catches every sound, a 16-stage
diffuser smears it, and four cascading stages multiply it into as many as 6,561 overlapping copies.
All of that pours into a 16-line "bloom" network that can sustain for a full minute. The attack
dissolves: you don't hear your pluck with a tail on it, you hear a drone growing out of it.

**Density** does most of the work. It stretches the cloud from a 6-second swell to a 60-second
wash, and the output level stays matched the whole way, so you can ride it like a fader.
**Time** runs from 20 ms to 20 s. It crossfades instead of stretching the delay, so sweeping it
never bends the pitch. Push **Feedback** to 100% for infinite repeats, or hit **Freeze** to hold
the cloud while you play over it.

**Spin** sets the motion: every delay line drifts slowly in pitch, from a subtle shimmer to a deep,
tape-like wobble. **Tone** sets the colour, with a smooth dark-to-bright tilt and no resonant
peaks. Six models give six shapes: **Drift** models build slowly into a vast cloud, and **Surge**
models swell up fast and stream out.

It all sits in a warm amber-white interface with dark knobs. A live echo-pattern display shows
where the copies land in time, and a level ring glows around the Density knob.

## Features

- Turns any input (plucks, keys, pads, voice, guitar, field recordings) into a sustained, smooth drone
- Up to 6,561 overlapping, slowly drifting copies, plus a 16-line bloom with up to 60 s of decay
- **Density**: one knob from a 6 s swell to a 60 s wash, level-matched across the range
- **Time**: 20 ms – 20 s, pitch-free changes (crossfade, no tape-style bending)
- **Feedback** up to infinite, plus **Freeze** to hold the cloud
- **Spin**: from gentle shimmer to deep, wobbly drift
- **Tone**: smooth dark ↔ bright tilt with no resonances
- **6 models**: Drift and Surge, each in Large, Medium and Small
- Equal-power **Mix**, stereo **Width** (0–150%), Input and Output gain, bypass
- **14 factory presets**, plus your own user presets
- Echo-pattern display, level ring, and hover help for every control in the status bar
- Resizable interface (70–160%) with smooth, slightly weighted knobs
- Light on CPU: about 6–7% of one core in testing

## Specs

- Formats: VST3 (64-bit) and Standalone app
- System: Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit
- Mono or stereo in, stereo out
- Reports a 60 s tail to the host, so long drones aren't cut off at the end of a render
